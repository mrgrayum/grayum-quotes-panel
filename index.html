Daily Quotes Panel — Firmware Installer

For the SeeedStudio XIAO 7.5" ePaper Panel (ESP32-C3). Firmware 0.1.0.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (the installer needs Web Serial — it won't work in Safari, Firefox, or on a phone).
  2. Plug the panel into the computer with a USB-C cable.
  3. Click Connect below, pick the panel's serial port, then click Install. If it asks, let it erase the device — this is a first install.
  4. Wait for "Installation complete," then close the installer. The panel restarts itself.
This browser doesn't support Web Serial — please open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Serial access was blocked — reload the page and allow the serial port prompt.
Panel doesn't show up? Unplug it, hold the panel's small BOOT button while plugging the USB cable back in, click Connect, and release the button once the port appears / the installer connects.

After flashing — WiFi setup

  1. The firmware ships with no WiFi saved. To open its setup hotspot right away, power-cycle the panel three times in a row (unplug / replug USB three times, a few seconds apart).
  2. On your phone, join the WiFi network WaltQuotes-Setup. The setup page should pop up on its own; if it doesn't, browse to 192.168.4.1.
  3. Type your home WiFi name and password and tap Save & reconnect. The panel reboots, connects, syncs its clock, and pulls up today's quote. (Before Dec 1 it fetches the quote live; the preloaded year starts Dec 1, 2026.)

The quote on screen never changes during setup, and if the panel ever loses WiFi for three wake-ups in a row it re-opens the same setup hotspot on its own.
